# Hamburg Theater Venues: Landscape & Ticketing Research

This document provides a comprehensive, currently-operating list of Hamburg theater venues (live performing-arts stages with their own programming), organized by category, along with their official websites, ticketing systems, programming/size profiles, and Wikidata Q-ids.

## Sprechtheater / Schauspiel (Drama Stages)

* **Deutsches Schauspielhaus** (Hamburgisches Staatsschauspiel)
  * **URL:** [schauspielhaus.de](https://www.schauspielhaus.de)
  * **Category:** Sprechtheater / Schauspiel
  * **Ticketing:** Reservix
  * **Profile:** Germany's largest speech theater stage, ~1,200 seats in the Main House (Großes Haus). Landmark state-subsidized theater.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q452077

* **Thalia Theater**
  * **URL:** [thalia-theater.de](https://www.thalia-theater.de)
  * **Category:** Sprechtheater / Schauspiel
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Eventim backend)
  * **Profile:** Major state-subsidized theater focusing on classical and contemporary drama, ~1,000 seats.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q459892

* **Thalia Gaußstraße** (Thalia in der Gaußstraße)
  * **URL:** [thalia-theater.de/programm/spielstaetten/gaussstrasse](https://www.thalia-theater.de/programm/spielstaetten/gaussstrasse)
  * **Category:** Sprechtheater / Schauspiel
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Eventim backend)
  * **Profile:** Studio stage of Thalia Theater in Altona, ~200 seats, focused on experimental and contemporary plays.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q2410309

* **Ernst Deutsch Theater**
  * **URL:** [ernst-deutsch-theater.de](https://www.ernst-deutsch-theater.de)
  * **Category:** Sprechtheater / Schauspiel
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Comfortticket)
  * **Profile:** Germany's largest private speaker's theater, ~740 seats, classical and modern dramas.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q1357876

* **Altonaer Theater**
  * **URL:** [altonaer-theater.de](https://www.altonaer-theater.de)
  * **Category:** Sprechtheater / Schauspiel
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Reservix backend)
  * **Profile:** Large private theater in Altona, ~500 seats, focusing on literary adaptations, novels, and historical plays.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q444211

* **Hamburger Kammerspiele**
  * **URL:** [hamburger-kammerspiele.de](https://www.hamburger-kammerspiele.de)
  * **Category:** Sprechtheater / Schauspiel
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Reservix backend)
  * **Profile:** Renowned private theater in Rotherbaum, ~400 seats, focusing on contemporary drama and socially critical plays.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q1573215

* **Ohnsorg-Theater**
  * **URL:** [ohnsorg.de](https://www.ohnsorg.de)
  * **Category:** Sprechtheater / Schauspiel
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Reservix backend)
  * **Profile:** Famous regional theater performing plays in Low German (Plattdeutsch), ~400 seats.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q2042322

* **St. Pauli Theater**
  * **URL:** [st-pauli-theater.de](https://www.st-pauli-theater.de)
  * **Category:** Sprechtheater / Schauspiel
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Eventim backend)
  * **Profile:** Hamburg's oldest private theater on the Reeperbahn, ~350 seats, staging modern dramas, comedies, and cabaret.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q884102

* **Harburger Theater**
  * **URL:** [harburger-theater.de](https://www.harburger-theater.de)
  * **Category:** Sprechtheater / Schauspiel
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Reservix backend)
  * **Profile:** Historic regional theater in Hamburg-Harburg, staging drama, comedy, and classics in a ~500-seat hall.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q1584880

* **LichtwarkTheater**
  * **URL:** [theater-bergedorf.de](https://www.theater-bergedorf.de)
  * **Category:** Sprechtheater / Schauspiel
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Stäitsch Theaterbetriebs system)
  * **Profile:** Modern theater stage at the KörberHaus in Bergedorf, ~458 seats, hosting touring productions, theater, and concerts.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** None (shares location with KörberHaus)

## Oper (Opera)

* **Staatsoper Hamburg** (Hamburgische Staatsoper)
  * **URL:** [staatsoper-hamburg.de](https://www.staatsoper-hamburg.de)
  * **Category:** Oper
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Eventim backend)
  * **Profile:** Major state opera house, ~1,690 seats, home of the Hamburg State Opera, Hamburg Ballett, and Philharmonic State Orchestra.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q689849

* **Hamburger Kammeroper** (shares Allee Theater building)
  * **URL:** [alleetheater.de](https://www.alleetheater.de)
  * **Category:** Oper
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (Reservix / Eventim backend)
  * **Profile:** Small opera house in Altona sharing the Allee Theater venue, specializing in classic operas in German translation, ~200 seats.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q1573210

* **Opernloft**
  * **URL:** [opernloft.de](https://www.opernloft.de)
  * **Category:** Oper
  * **Ticketing:** Reservix
  * **Profile:** Unique opera house at the Altona Ferry Terminal, staging condensed, modern 90-minute opera productions, ~200 seats.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q2026779

## Ballett & zeitgenössischer Tanz (Dance Stages)

* **Kampnagel**
  * **URL:** [kampnagel.de](https://www.kampnagel.de)
  * **Category:** Ballett & zeitgenössischer Tanz
  * **Ticketing:** Own webshop (artwork ticketing software)
  * **Profile:** Germany's largest production center for contemporary performing arts, 6 stages, up to ~850 seats.
  * **Wikidata Q-id:** Q1723223

*(Note: The Hamburg Ballett and Bundesjugendballett are listed below under the Touring-Only/Stage-Less section as they perform at the Staatsoper Hamburg or other guest venues and do not run their own physical theater venues with distinct ticketing systems.)*
